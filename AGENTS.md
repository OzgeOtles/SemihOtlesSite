# UI changes

- Do not commit or amend without the user's explicit approval. Prepare changes for review first.

- Keep every section responsive on mobile, tablet and desktop.
- Check narrow screens (320–390 px), tablet (768 px), and desktop (at least 1280 px) for meaningful UI changes.
- Prevent page-wide horizontal overflow. Wide charts and tables may scroll inside their own labelled containers.
- Keep touch controls usable, long titles readable, and book cards sized to their content.
- Use the user-supplied source documents for publication statistics. Keep data-audit notes out of the visitor interface; report uncertainties in chat.
